Description:
Peptide Tracker is a private tracking companion for people who want a clearer record of peptide routines.

Log each shot with medication, dose, date, time, and injection site. Review recent entries, check your calendar, monitor weight and goal progress, and view half-life based level estimates for supported medications.

Built for simple daily use:
- Log shots quickly with dose, site, date, and time
- Track multiple peptides and active medications
- See estimated level trends based on half-life settings
- Review a calendar of logged shot days
- Track weight, goal weight, total change, and percent change
- Keep a clean history of shots and weight entries
- Use front and back body-site maps for rotation context

Peptide Tracker is for personal record keeping only. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, treatment guidance, dosage recommendations, or emergency support. Always follow guidance from a licensed clinician.
