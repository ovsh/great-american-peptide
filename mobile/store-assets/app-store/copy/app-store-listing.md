## Review Notes Draft

Peptide Tracker is a private manual logging tool for peptide routines and research calculation support. The app lets users manually record entries, body-site context, dates, times, weight, and goal weight. Estimated level charts are based on user-entered timing and half-life settings and are presented for personal tracking only. The reconstitution calculator is a mathematical utility for laboratory researchers and scientists: it converts user-entered vial mass and diluent volume into concentration values, with an optional neutral aliquot-volume conversion. The app does not recommend dosing, diagnose conditions, provide treatment guidance, provide administration instructions, or replace professional medical care.
