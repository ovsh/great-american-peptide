# Competitor ad creative — MeAgain and the GLP-1 persona-diary format

Teardown of 32 archived TikTok posts, Aug 2026. Working copies and `index.csv` are in
`research/meagain-tiktok/` — gitignored, local only. That material is **MeAgain's and
Gala's copyright**: study it, never republish it, and never put a frame of it in Poke's
marketing. Downloading it conflicts with TikTok's terms of service (a contract matter,
not a criminal one); saving public competitor ads for internal study is ordinary
practice, but that is the trade-off. TikTok Creative Center has no advertiser filter,
so there was no sanctioned route to the same material.

## Correct the record first

`market.md` says "35+ accounts, 9 still live". That number is **credible**, but the word
"farm" undersells the mechanic. What is actually verifiable:

| | Accounts | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| MeAgain-owned | 1 | `@meagain.app`, bio links meagain.com |
| MeAgain confirmed | 6 | the MeAgain UI is **visible on screen** in the video |
| Same cluster, no brand in the sampled post | 5 | `<name>onzepbound` naming + near-identical bios |
| Gala partner network | 3 | `@galahealth` / `@galaglp1` named in 41–83% of their posts |

Two findings that cost time and are worth inheriting:

1. **Bios and captions are a weak signal; on-screen app footage is the strong one.** A
   caption grep found 1 MeAgain account. Extracting frames and looking found 6. Four
   accounts I had written off as unattributed were showing MeAgain's own UI.
2. **The persona-diary playbook is category-wide, not MeAgain's invention.** Gala runs a
   bigger version of it. Do not treat this as one competitor's trick.

`@meagain_the_glp1_company`, the handle on MeAgain's own website, is dead.

## Length — this is the headline

| Format | n | Range | Median |
|---|---|---|---|
| Video | 26 | 4–53s | **7s** |
| Slideshow | 5 | 12–65s | 33s |

**21 of 26 videos (81%) are 9 seconds or shorter.** The 4–9s band *is* the format. Long
form exists only as photo slideshows. Two outliers (43s, 53s) are ordinary creators
talking to camera, not cluster posts.

Views are small — median a few hundred, most under 1,000. The one hit (83.4K) is
MeAgain's own account. This model does not win on any single video; it wins on volume.

## Hooks

Every hook is a **finished thought, not a question** — a conclusion offered as personal
discovery. Four shapes, in rough order of how often they appear:

- **Bait-and-switch.** `I can't see any glp1 effect:` → cut → `THE effect`. Also
  `Shot day was yesterday. Can you tell??`
- **Unfinished sentence.** `One thing that's surprised me since starting with Gala...`
  The ellipsis holds the viewer for the payoff card.
- **Third-person gossip.** `they did NOT let her in the club bc drivers license "was not
  hers" — INSANE TRANSFORMATION 😭`
- **Complaint as intimacy.** `Day 35 on zepbound - STILL CONSTIPATED 😭 but my glp1 pet is
  begging me to eat so I hit my protein goal`. `kinda fell off the wagon there for a sec..
  we back at it 😩`

Text is one or two static cards, not per-word animated captions. A card usually holds for
most of the runtime. Typos survive to publication (`fods`) — nobody is polishing these.

## Structure

The 7-second cluster post is a two-shot:

1. **0–3s, sensory bait.** A face very close to the lens, eating, or food filling the
   frame. Unrelated to the product claim. Hook card up from frame one.
2. **3–7s, the reveal.** Cut to a hand holding a phone. The app carries the payoff.

The Gala variant drops step 2 entirely — hook card, payoff card, no product ever shown.
That is how one account ships 83 of them.

## How the app appears in frame

Never a full-screen screen recording. Four treatments, all worth stealing:

- **Hand-held phone in the real scene.** The phone enters a shot already containing the
  thing being logged — food on the table stays visible beside the screen. MeAgain's best
  post is one continuous move: pancakes → scanner viewfinder → `Log Meal / Pancakes with
  Strawberries / Protein 17.60g` → dashboard. A feature demo dressed as a breakfast.
- **Home-screen widget.** The capybara pet widget on a real, cluttered home screen
  alongside other apps. Cheapest possible proof the app lives on a real phone.
- **App-icon sticker.** TikTok's own icon sticker pinned top-right, so the name is
  findable in the store without a spoken CTA.
- **Screenshot sticker with the creator's handle stamped on it.** Proof the data is
  theirs.

The UI reads at roughly a third of frame height, angled, in hand — never flat or
pixel-perfect. Screen legibility is deliberately traded for the impression of a real phone.

## What we must not copy

The deception is specific and documented. Cluster accounts present as one person's diary
(`Just a girl on her zepbound journey`) while the video is third-person (`was not hers`).
Across two accounts with **different** "before" subjects, the "after" subject appears to
be the **same person**, and the numbers do not survive contact: BMI 19.4 in one post and
24.2 in another for a body that looks identical; a "before" at 225 lb / BMI 32.3 and an
"after" at 169 lb / BMI 24.2 sold as −66 lb. Real people's bodies are dressed as somebody
else's progress. That is the whole trick, and it is off limits — the App Store review
guidelines and the FTC endorsement rules both bite here, and `AGENTS.md` already forbids
placeholder or fake data in a shipping screen. The same rule applies to our ads.

Also skip: partner tags (`#meagainpartner`) used as if they were ad disclosure — they are
not. And note `@jess.glp1.journey` runs a 35–51% like-to-view rate when everything else in
the corpus sits at 0.3–8.6%. That is bought engagement. It buys nothing real.

## What is legitimate for Poke

- **Ship 4–9 second ads.** Our instinct will be 30s. The category has already settled the
  question.
- **Lead with a conclusion, not a question.** `I can't see any glp1 effect:` → the effect.
- **Bait with food, pay off with the log.** Our scanner and protein ring do this natively.
- **Show the phone in the room**, in hand, beside the real object. Not a screen recording.
- **Put the widget on a real home screen.** Poke has a pet and a widget; this is a free
  format.
- **Use our own data or a consenting user's**, and say which. The honest version of the
  reveal shot is the whole ad, and it needs no fake body.
- **Slideshows for anything over 15 seconds.** Every long post in the corpus is one.
- **Assume volume, not hits.** Median a few hundred views. One perfect video is not a plan.
