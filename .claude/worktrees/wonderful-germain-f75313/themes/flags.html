Flag backdrop — 5 directions

Each variant uses the same hero copy and credentials rail. Only the backdrop treatment changes. Click a card to open full-size.

A  Silk Ghost
Full-color flag, 10% opacity, feathered left-to-right. Quietest, most editorial.
B  Ken Burns Cinema
Dark cinematic. Flag pans slowly under a navy wash. White hero type inverts on top.
C  Treasury Engraving
Navy crosshatch over a cream base. Reads as currency / banknote / federal document.
D  Abstracted Stripes
Giant tonal horizontal bands, canton in the corner. The flag as composition, not picture.
E  Split Stage
50/50. Left: dark, dramatic, flag cropped tight. Right: clean paper with the CoA. High contrast.
