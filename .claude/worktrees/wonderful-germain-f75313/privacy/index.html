Peptide Tracker Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 30, 2026. Peptide Tracker is designed as a private, on-device record keeping app.

Summary

Peptide Tracker does not require an account, does not include third-party advertising, and is not designed to track you across apps or websites. Shot logs, medication entries, injection sites, estimated levels, weight entries, goal weight, and app preferences are stored on your device unless a future feature clearly asks you to enable syncing or export.

Information stored in the app

You may choose to enter medication names, doses, injection dates and times, injection sites, weight entries, goal weight, reminder preferences, and related notes or settings. This information is used to show your logs, calendar, progress, and estimated level charts inside the app.

Data collection

At launch, Peptide Tracker does not collect app data on our servers and does not include analytics or advertising SDKs. Apple explains that data processed only on device and not sent off device is not considered collected for App Store privacy details. If this changes, this policy and the App Store privacy label will be updated before the changed behavior is released.

Support emails

If you email support, we receive the email address and information you choose to include in the message. We use that information to respond to your request and troubleshoot the issue. Do not send sensitive medical information unless you decide it is necessary for your support request.

Reminders and notifications

If you enable reminders, notification scheduling is used to remind you about logged routines. Notification permissions are controlled by iOS settings.

Health data

If a future version offers Apple Health integration, the app will ask for permission before reading or writing Health data. Health data will be used only for the app features you enable.

Data deletion

You can delete entries inside the app where deletion controls are provided. You can also remove app data by deleting the app from your device. For support questions about data deletion, email contact@peptide.industries.

Children

Peptide Tracker is not intended for children. The app is intended for adults using it as a personal record keeping tool.

Medical disclaimer

Peptide Tracker is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, treatment guidance, dosage recommendations, or emergency support. Always follow guidance from a licensed clinician.

Contact

For privacy questions, email contact@peptide.industries.
